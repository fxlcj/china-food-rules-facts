# China Food Rules & Facts — verified rules with sources and check dates

A reference for the food rules that visitors to China most often trip on: how ordering works, what you may be charged, how QR and cash payment behave, what food labels must tell you, and what official designations stand behind the dishes you see.

Every row carries **the official Chinese source that states it** and **the date it was last checked**. If you copy a figure anywhere, copy the check date with it — that is what makes the number verifiable at all.

- `facts.json` — the same 39 entries, machine-readable

- The human-readable version, with worked examples: <https://jiunotes.top/en/food/>

- How entries are kept current: see [Maintenance](#maintenance)

## Why this exists

English-language guides to eating in China are usually written once, from memory, and then age silently. Tip advice gets recycled from other countries. Menu fees get quoted as if they were universal. Portion and allergen rules get described as if every shop were identical.

The rules themselves are not secret — China's national legislature, regulators and standard bodies publish them, in Chinese. The gap is that almost nobody ties the English sentence back to the Chinese source, or says when they last looked. This file exists to close that gap.

**Scope, and a note on honesty.** These are published rules, standards and official designations. They are not travel advice, they are not a booking service, and this is not an official publication. Where a rule could not be confirmed against an official page, it is not listed here — a shorter list beats a guessed number. Tipping is deliberately absent: China has no official no-tipping rule to cite, so the customary advice is not dressed up as law.

## What is *not* in this file

- **Live prices.** Menu prices move with city, venue and season; only a specific posted price on the day is real, and no national figure exists.

- **Tipping.** China has no legal rule requiring or prohibiting a tip; custom varies, so no row is invented to fill the gap.

- **Subjective 'best of' lists.** Rankings of dishes or cities without an official designation behind them are opinion, not fact.

- **Anything we could not verify.** If no official Chinese page states it, it is not here.

## Maintenance

This dataset is not written once and abandoned:

- Every source URL is re-fetched on a monthly maintenance run. When a page's content changes, the entry is flagged and the claim/figure is updated against the new official text with a CHANGELOG line.

- Each entry carries a `checked_on` date in `facts.json`. If a figure changes, its date changes with it — a number is never quietly swapped while the old date stays in place.
